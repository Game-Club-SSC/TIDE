# Issue #112 Notes: Non-GDD Scope Candidates

This note captures candidate systems that appear beyond current GDD-required vertical-slice scope, and why they can be deferred.

## Candidate defer list

- In-battle party swap convenience paths (GDD emphasizes between-battle party composition).
- Cosmetic progression economy/style pathways not required by core loop acceptance.
- Extra combat breadth beyond elemental advantage, clash, momentum, and Tide Break basics.
- Expanded map zoom/marker complexity not required to validate island restoration loop.
- Advanced overworld roaming AI complexity beyond reliable encounter triggering.

## Reasoning

Deferring these systems reduces regression surface while keeping the milestone focused on documented core-loop delivery.
