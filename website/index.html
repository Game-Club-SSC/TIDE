The central mechanic

Move light. Move shadow. Restore balance.

Every corrupted area exists somewhere between excess shadow and excess light. Take Tide from one place, carry it across the grid, and place it where the island needs it most.

Tide scale

Five is not neutral.
Five is harmony.

Values below five lean into shadow. Values above five distort toward overwhelming light.

01 · EXCESS SHADOW05 · BALANCE10 · EXCESS LIGHT
5 is safe. The system rewards restoring balance, not simply pushing every tile toward “good”.
How the game flows

An island is not conquered. It is restored.

Combat can clear corruption, but it cannot complete an island alone. Players explore, solve Tide puzzles, and reach the restoration threshold before confronting each boss.

01ExploreRead the island and find corrupted areas.
02RebalanceMove Tide between tiles and regions.
03UnsealDefeat enemies guarding blocked spaces.
04RestorePush island restoration toward the threshold.
05ConfrontUnlock and defeat the island boss.
06Sail onCarry the party forward to the next island.
Five forces

Every advantage creates another vulnerability.

Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and Space form a closed elemental system. No hero is universally strongest; the party succeeds through composition, timing, and balance.

FIRE

Pressure, heat, and decisive offense.

Strong: Earth + Air
Weak: Water + Space

WATER

Flow, adaptation, and control.

Strong: Fire + Space
Weak: Earth + Air

EARTH

Stability, weight, and endurance.

Strong: Water + Space
Weak: Fire + Air

AIR

Speed, mobility, and momentum.

Strong: Earth + Water
Weak: Fire + Space

SPACE

Position, distance, and disruption.

Strong: Fire + Air
Weak: Water + Earth

The story beneath the adventure

The closer the world comes to balance, the closer the heroes come to the truth.

TIDE begins as an elemental fantasy journey and gradually becomes a story about fate, purpose, attachment, and the cost of completing the task you were born to do.

01

Emotional inevitability

The story moves toward acceptance of an unavoidable fate—not a simple rebellion against it.

02

Balance over power

The systems reward judgement, restraint, and restoration instead of domination.

03

Character attachment

The party must feel worth caring about before the ending can carry its full weight.

Six corrupted islands

A voyage measured in restoration.

Each island combines exploration, encounters, sealed puzzle spaces, and a boss that appears only once enough of the island has been returned to balance.
